## Short description (under 160 characters)

> RRoyal (RRx) is the payment token for the RRoyal delivery app on Cronos. Pay for orders with RRx or stablecoins.

## Long description

> RRoyal (RRx) is a fixed-supply token on the Cronos chain, built to pay for orders in the RRoyal
> delivery app. Customers can check out with RRx or a stablecoin, and every payment is settled on chain.
>
> The RRx contract is simple and transparent: 100,000,000 tokens were created once at launch, nobody
> can mint more, and there is no owner or admin control over balances. Holders can burn their own
> tokens. The source code is verified on the Cronos Explorer, and the RRx / CRO liquidity on
> VVS Finance is locked in a public time-lock contract.

## Wording to avoid

Don't describe RRx as an investment or promise price gains, returns, or "passive income". Describe
what it does (payments in the app), not what it might be worth.
